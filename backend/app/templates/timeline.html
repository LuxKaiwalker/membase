Timeline

Memory in time

Episodes in order, newest first. A date is shown only as precisely as it is actually known, and memories whose date isn't known at all are listed at the end rather than filed under a day that was guessed for them.

{# A real key, not a legend for decoration: the three boundary states and the precision labels below are the difference between "you told me this happened in July 2023" and "a model read a date out of your note". Collapsed by default so it does not crowd the timeline itself. #}
How to read this timeline
dates stated
You wrote the dates yourself. The span is your own claim.
dates extracted
Every memory here has a date, but those dates were read out of your notes rather than entered as dates. The span follows from them.
span open
At least one memory in the episode has no date at all, so the real episode may extend past the dates shown. The span covers only the memories that do have one.

Precision. "July 2023" means the month is known and the day is not; "2021" means only the year is. Nothing on this page shows a day, month or season that isn't actually recorded — and an episode with no resolvable date is shown as undated, never placed at a position chosen for it.

Titles are composed, not written. An episode's title is built from its most frequent people, places and organizations plus its date span. It is a label, not a summary of what happened.

The connector between two episodes is scaled to the time between them, so a long gap reads as a gap. Gaps are computed from each span's representative anchor date and reported at the coarser of the two precisions.
