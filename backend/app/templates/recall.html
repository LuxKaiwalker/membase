Recall

Start from whatever you remember

A half-remembered fragment is enough to begin. membase searches what you have stored, explores the connections around what it finds, and asks you one concrete question at a time when several memories are still plausible -- rather than guessing. Everything it puts forward comes with the evidence behind it, and with how it got there.
